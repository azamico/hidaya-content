# Hidaya privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Hidaya is made by Azam Haq. This policy covers the Hidaya apps for iPhone and Android.

## The short version

Hidaya collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you do in the
app stays on your phone.

## What stays on your phone

- **Location.** If you choose automatic location, Hidaya reads your approximate location to work out prayer times and
  the Qibla direction, and names it after the nearest city using a list built into the app. If you turn on "Update when
  I travel", the phone checks your location in the background for the same purpose. Your location is never sent
  anywhere.
- **Your settings and reading data**: prayer settings, bookmarks, notes, highlights, khatm progress, the prayer
  tracker, tasbih counts and similar. They are stored only on your phone (and in your device backups, which Apple or
  Google keep for you under your own account).

## What the app downloads

- **Content packs**: Quran translations and hadith collections you choose to download come from a public file host
  (GitHub). The request contains only the file name, like any web download; nothing about you is sent.
- **Recitation audio** is streamed from EveryAyah (everyayah.com) when you press play. Their server sees an ordinary
  request for the audio file.

## Purchases

The free trial is counted on your phone. Purchases (the yearly subscription and the lifetime unlock) are handled
entirely by the App Store or Google Play under your Apple or Google account. Hidaya never sees your payment details and
has no server.

## Permissions

Each permission is asked for only when you turn on the feature that needs it: location (automatic location, travel
updates), notifications (prayer alerts and reminders), exact alarms (alerts on time, Android), and setting the
wallpaper (the daily lock screen, Android).

## Children

Hidaya is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at
https://github.com/azamico/hidaya-content/issues.
